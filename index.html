ON MY BIRTHDAY

I can start with 100. Brands can help make it much bigger.

I’m only 17, so I don’t want to pretend I can fund something huge by myself. My starting plan is simple: use my own money to give out 100 burgers on my birthday. If brands sponsor the available placements, I can put more of that support into food, packaging and distribution — which could turn 100 meals into hundreds or potentially 1,000+ depending on how much sponsorship comes in.
