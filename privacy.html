Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This policy explains what information may be collected through the birthday sponsorship website and how it may be used.

Information you provide

When you create or purchase a sponsor profile, the site may collect information such as:

  • Brand or sponsor name.
  • Logo or profile image.
  • Short brand description.
  • Website URL and X/Twitter handle.
  • Spot selection and sponsorship amount.
  • Email or contact information if required for payment confirmation, receipts, refunds or takeover notifications.

Payment information

When real checkout is enabled, payment data will be handled by Dodo Payments or another clearly disclosed payment provider. This website is not intended to store full card numbers, CVVs or payment credentials.

How information is used

  • To create and display your public sponsor profile.
  • To confirm and manage the spots you own.
  • To process takeover, payment and refund-related communication.
  • To prepare sponsor logos for real-world packaging or clothing placements.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.

Public sponsor information

Your submitted brand name, logo, description, X handle, website and sponsor placement are intended to be public. Do not submit information you do not want displayed publicly.

Analytics

The site may collect basic analytics such as page visits, sponsor-profile views, clicks and general device/browser information. These analytics are used to understand how the project and sponsor placements are performing.

Data sharing

Personal information is not sold to advertisers. Data may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the project, such as hosting, analytics, storage and payment processing.

Data retention and requests

Project and payment-related records may be kept for reasonable operational, fraud-prevention, accounting or legal purposes. You may ask about, correct or request deletion of eligible personal information by contacting Ayush.
